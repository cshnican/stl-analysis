Sihan · 10 gold, 8 silver, 8 bronze

A gold medal is first place, a silver medal is second, and a bronze medal is third. Places follow the Eurovision tie-break: more points, then more 12s, then 10s, then 8s, and on through 1. If the whole card matches, the song earlier in the running order places higher. Where votes from 7 down to 1 were not saved, the countback stops after the 8s. Heads of delegation are ordered by gold, then silver, then bronze. Equal medal counts share a rank. One edition can give the same person more than one medal.
