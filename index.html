Share the Love

Share the Love (STL) is a monthly, Eurovision-style fan contest. Each participant acts as a Head of Delegation (HoD) and selects two countries to represent. Everyone then listens to the competing songs and votes for their favorites using the Eurovision points system: 12 points for first place, 10 for second, and so on. The song with the highest total score wins, and its country earns the right to host the next edition.

This website serves as a walkthrough of the contest: the scoreboard of every edition, with its ranking, points, and ballots, and a look at where the songs come from, by country and by head of delegation.
